# Creative direction — find, show and prove the governing idea

Translate strategy into an expressive idea that can generate many coherent decisions. Do not force the first plausible direction to explain itself into permanence, and do not commit a direction nobody has seen.

## 1. Start from decision-changing material

Use the brand job, offer truth, desired meaning, right to win, relevant history/equity, audience context and real constraints as raw material.

A direction should be traceable through a short causal lineage:

**brand truth → creative insight → expressive mechanism → consequence in use**

The lineage is not presentation copy. It is a test against post-rationalization: if the expressive mechanism cannot be connected to true, particular material from this brand, the route is style looking for a reason.

## 2. Build a small reference field

Use research to widen the search space before committing, not to decorate a moodboard. Stop when new references no longer change a decision or expose a new search region.

Cover only the regions that can change the work:
- **category field** — current competitors, conventions, useful codes and clichés; this reveals the competent default and what must be earned rather than inherited;
- **craft bar** — two or three real identities from adjacent categories whose execution this work must hold beside;
- **outside field** — other categories, editorial, products, architecture, culture, objects, art, technology or systems with a transferable mechanism relevant to the problem.

Only references that can be inspected now qualify. Decompose each useful reference into transferable properties such as proportion, rhythm, density, framing, typography, materiality, motion, verbal behavior or another mechanism. References contribute mechanisms, never surfaces to copy.

## 3. Name the floor and the bar

The **floor** is the most available competent-but-generic answer to this brief or category. Ask what a capable designer would produce from category autocomplete alone, which parts of a proposed direction would survive if the company name were swapped for a competitor's, and what in this brief earns any familiar device in use. Do not invert the default mechanically; familiar forms remain valid when causally justified.

The **bar** is the strongest inspectable work named in the reference field. If no real identity can be inspected, state the bar as observable properties instead of names. Decompose what makes the bar strong with the force mechanisms in `identity-craft.md` so comparison is about mechanism, not taste. Boards are judged against the bar, not against the brief alone; a board that only clears the floor has not cleared anything.

## 4. Diverge by independent lineage, not cosmetics

A route is materially different only when its governing idea or generative grammar changes. Font, color, logo-shape or layout swaps inside the same idea are variations, not new routes.

Explore through structurally different sources: tensions, behaviors, histories, materials, product mechanics, language, rituals, spatial logics, systems or other relevant mechanisms. Do not require every sketch to carry a full rationale.

When isolated contexts or subagents are available, use them to generate lineages independently from the same FRAME before synthesis. Each branch should ignore rival solutions while generating. Isolation reduces anchoring; it is an optimization, not a runtime dependency, and the studio must still work without it.

Keep exploration disposable. Do not persist reference fields, rejected routes, branch transcripts or prompts in canonical state.

## 5. Art-direct outside the generator role

Do not let the generator grade its own first idea into permanence. After divergence, change role and compare only surviving lineages against non-compensatory gates:
- **grounding** — does the route depend on true, particular material from this brand?
- **relevance** — does it solve the actual brand job?
- **evident logic** — does the relationship become intelligible in the work without explanatory rescue?
- **distinction** — is the difference perceptible without gratuitous novelty?
- **generativity** — can the mechanism create coherent range rather than one image?
- **craft potential** — can the route plausibly hold beside the bar in the required media?

Novelty does not compensate for weak grounding. Polish does not compensate for a generic idea. A persuasive rationale does not compensate for a route whose logic is absent from the artifact.

## 6. End exploration rendered

Each lineage that survives art direction becomes a board: a composed visual argument, not a list of adjectives. A board shows the signature at full force in one hero moment on a declared touchpoint, the typographic voice on real words, the color relationship at scale, and one dense application next to one expressive one. Generated imagery may be used here as exploration, whether or not final assets will be generated; load `visual-production.md` when it is.

Make the boards comparable: same touchpoints, same content, different ideas. Judge what the boards actually produce. A direction can discover a stronger framing than the initial verbal strategy; it may refine creative interpretation, but it may not invent business truth.

## 7. Find a thesis with generative force

A useful creative thesis is:
- narrower than the whole strategy;
- richer than a mood adjective;
- specific enough to affect real choices;
- broad enough to work across more than one medium;
- capable of producing variation without losing family resemblance.

Avoid empty destinations such as `modern`, `premium`, `innovative`, `trustworthy`, `bold` or `human` unless the thesis explains what behavior makes the quality perceptible.

## 8. Present, then earn commitment

Present the strongest boards with a recommendation and the reasons, plus the names the system's elements will carry.

In **recommend-and-ratify**, the owner ratifies or redirects before BUILD SYSTEM. In **co-direct**, expose the same evidence early enough for collaboration to change the commitment rather than decorate it afterward. In explicit **delegate** mode, the studio may choose the strongest proven route and continue without asking for preference.

Commit a working direction when it demonstrates:
- clear dependence on this brand;
- strategic relevance;
- evident internal logic;
- distinctiveness without gratuitous novelty;
- craft that holds beside the named bar;
- ability to generate a system rather than one hero image;
- compatibility with existing equity that still matters;
- enough range to survive the declared touchpoints;
- plausible production feasibility.

At commitment time, compress the durable direction to **thesis**, **principles**, **signature** and, when needed, **excludes**. Preserve the mechanism, not the exploration transcript.

## 9. Know when to abandon the direction

Re-diverge when representative use shows that the thesis:
- produces generic/category-coded work;
- needs constant exceptions;
- cannot generate enough range;
- relies on polish, novelty, symbolism or rationale to seem strong;
- conflicts with real content/touchpoints;
- cannot explain recurring system failures.

Refine instead when the thesis remains strong and the defect is downstream craft or execution.
